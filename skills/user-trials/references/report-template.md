# User Trials report

Keep the default report compact while retaining reproducible evidence.

## Trial charter

- Product/version and tested surface
- Missions and safety boundaries
- Panel and why each persona contributes unique coverage
- Protocol mode: delegated or sequential fallback

## Persona results

For each persona:

- **Situation:** role, goal, context, permissions, success criterion
- **Outcome:** completed, failed, abandoned, or inconclusive
- **Experience trace:** expectation -> action -> observation -> evidence
- **Friction and recovery:** task impact and severity
- **Opinion:** the persona's situated assessment
- **Confidence and limitations**

Severity describes mission impact:

- **Blocker:** the mission cannot be completed safely.
- **High:** completion produces a wrong result or requires an unsafe workaround.
- **Medium:** material delay, confusion, or recovery work.
- **Low:** localized friction or preference with little task impact.

## Cross-persona synthesis

- Corroborated observations
- Divergent reactions and the context that explains them
- Product implications framed as hypotheses
- Inconclusive areas and missing evidence
- If requested, an explicit evidence packet for Council; otherwise stop without
  forcing a product or architecture decision
